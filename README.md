# Heart-Disease-Prediction-using-Hybrid-Machine-Learning-Techniques

As heart disease is a leading cause of morbidity and death worldwide, early diagnosis and
treatment are important. Machine learning algorithms using a variety of clinical and
demographic data have been shown to effectively predict heart disease. Using a variety of
techniques, including logistic regression, decision trees, support vector machines, random
forests, and gradient boosting, we propose a machine learning-based heart disease line
estimation method in this study. To find the best specialists in cardiovascular disease, we
used a custom selection process that uses information on patient registries with a variety of
medical conditions and demographics. Our tests showed that Gradient Boost has the highest
accuracy in predicting heart disease, while all algorithms have the highest accuracy.
Performance evaluations of all algorithms are provided. We also identified the most
important markers of cardiovascular disease that may influence treatment decisions. This
method can help doctors identify heart disease and choose the right way to manage the
patient.
